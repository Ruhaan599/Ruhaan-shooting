How The Score Gets Made

Three things a 10-ring shot actually depends on.

Air pistol scoring rewards stillness more than speed. Underneath the scoresheet are three trained habits.

Pneumatic Weapon Control

Working with a .177-caliber compressed-air pistol at a fixed 10 metres, Ruhaan has trained trigger isolation to a fine point — a break clean enough that it doesn't disturb a sight picture that took seconds to build.

Breath & Pulse Control

A qualification round runs 60 shots long, and each one asks for a lowered resting heart rate on demand. Ruhaan works timed breath-holding windows and pulse-drop technique to hit that state shot after shot, not just once.

Composure Under The Clock

Air pistol is often called the most mentally punishing event in the sport — crowd noise, a hard countdown, one bad shot that can't be taken back. Ruhaan's record shows the recovery part: coming off a sub-optimal shot and holding the average anyway.
